Privacy Policy for 29 Card Game

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LogicMotion Studio ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use our mobile game 29 Card Game (the "Game") on Android.

1. Summary

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not run any servers. Your game data stays on your phone.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • "Play with friends" works only between phones on the same Wi-Fi network, directly, without going through the internet.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following only on your own phone, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us:

  • Your settings (music, sound and vibration).
  • The player name you type for "Play with friends" (optional).
  • A random device ID created by the Game, used only so you can rejoin a "Play with friends" game after a disconnection. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Your single-player statistics and win streaks.
  • A single-player match in progress, so you can continue it later.

You can delete all of this at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage in Android Settings. Statistics can also be reset from the Stats screen.

3. Playing with friends on the same Wi-Fi

When you host or join a "Play with friends" game, your phone talks directly to the other phones on the same local Wi-Fi network. It shares your player name, the Game's random device ID and your game moves with the other players in that game. To find nearby games, the Game sends short messages on the local network. None of this goes through our servers (we have none), and it is not stored after the game ends.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner during matches, an occasional full-screen ad between matches, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch for a hint. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version).
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): The first time you open the Game in these regions, Google's consent form lets you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time from More → Privacy options in the Game.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device). Ads will then no longer be personalised using it.

5. Other Google Play features

  • Ratings: The Game may ask once whether you'd like to rate it, using Google Play's in-app review. The review is handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Sharing: When you tap "Share", the Game creates a picture of your result or stats on your phone and passes it to the app you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

6. In-app purchases

The Game currently has no in-app purchases. If any are added in the future, payments will be handled by Google Play, and we will never see or store your payment details.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. "Play with friends" traffic stays on your local Wi-Fi network. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we keep data collection to the minimum the Game needs.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
