**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Information You Provide
We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to play the Game. The Game is a single-player offline experience and does not require a login.

### b) Automatically Collected Information
When you use the Game, we and our third-party service providers may automatically collect certain information, including:

- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with the Game, such as game progress, scores, and session duration.
- **Diagnostic Data**: Crash logs and performance data to help us improve the Game.

### c) Advertising and Analytics
If the Game includes advertisements, our advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may collect information to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. This may include device identifiers and usage data. You can learn more about how Google uses this data by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms.

We use the collected information to:
